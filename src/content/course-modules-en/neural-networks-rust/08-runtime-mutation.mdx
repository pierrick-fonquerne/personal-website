## What you will learn

- `MutateRandomSynapseWeight`: adding Gaussian noise to an existing weight
- `MutateRandomNeuronActivation`: swapping one activation for another
- Combining mutations and natural selection (evolutionary algorithms)

Gradient descent moves weights in a computed direction. Mutation moves them in a random one. Surprisingly, on some problem classes, that is competitive. We will see why and when.
