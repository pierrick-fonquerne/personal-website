## What you will learn

- Defining `enum Activation` with Identity, Sigmoid, ReLU, Tanh
- Implementing `apply(&self, x: f64) -> f64` and `derivative(&self, x: f64) -> f64`
- Comparing `enum` vs `trait object`: when to pick which

A canonical Rust design choice. The `enum` is simpler, faster, more predictable. The `trait object` buys you extensibility at the cost of dynamic dispatch. We will see how AEI-Framework decided.
