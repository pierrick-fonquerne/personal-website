## What you will learn

- The Event Sourcing pattern applied to a neural network
- `FileEventStore`: an append-only JSON Lines log
- Hydrating a network from its event history

A classical network serialises as a tensor. An event-sourced one serialises as a sequence of commands. The payoff: replay, debugging, audit, and the ability to fork a history to explore alternative branches.
