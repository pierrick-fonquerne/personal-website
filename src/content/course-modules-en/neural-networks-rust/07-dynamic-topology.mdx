## What you will learn

- The `AddRandomNeuron` command: pick, insert, rewire
- The `RemoveRandomNeuron` command: invariants to preserve
- Idempotence and determinism: why they are crucial under event sourcing

A classical network has a fixed topology. An evolving network changes shape while running. This chapter shows how to drive that change with typed commands rather than ad-hoc mutations.
