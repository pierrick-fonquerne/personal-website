## What you will learn

- Representing a network as `HashMap<Uuid, Neuron>` + `HashMap<Uuid, Synapse>`
- Topologically ordering neurons for propagation
- Handling cycles (or forbidding them) depending on the topology you allow

With AEI-Framework we leave the "layers" model and step into the "graph" model. More flexible, closer to biology, and it raises propagation questions we did not face before.
