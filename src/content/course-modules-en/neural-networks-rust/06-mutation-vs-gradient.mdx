## What you will learn

- Why the gradient isn't always the right answer
- Random mutation + selection: evolutionary algorithms striking back
- Curiosity-driven learning: letting the agent decide what to explore

Every ML framework runs gradient descent. AEI deliberately does something else. This chapter explains why: less efficient on well-defined problems in theory, more robust on ill-posed ones where the gradient does not even exist.
