## What you will learn

- The geometric intuition of a derivative: the slope of a curve at a point
- The chain rule for differentiating a composition of functions
- The extension to partial derivatives and gradients

Backpropagation is essentially the chain rule applied to a computation graph. This chapter gives you exactly what you need to follow the next one without getting stuck on the maths.
