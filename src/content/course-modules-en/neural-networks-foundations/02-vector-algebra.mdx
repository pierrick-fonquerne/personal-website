## What you will learn

- Representing a neuron's inputs as a vector
- The dot product: "weighted combination" in one notation
- Norms, distances, and what they mean geometrically

<Formula caption="Dot product">
x · w = x₁·w₁ + x₂·w₂ + ... + xₙ·wₙ
</Formula>

Every neural network is built on this operation. Understanding it geometrically is understanding what "learning" actually means in weight space.
