## What you will learn

- Why initialising all weights to zero does not work
- The Xavier-Glorot and He schemes, and when to use each
- The principle of batch normalisation and why it speeds convergence

Initialising a deep network is an art: a bad start can make it unable to learn. This chapter demystifies the choices that separate a network that converges in minutes from one that never learns.
