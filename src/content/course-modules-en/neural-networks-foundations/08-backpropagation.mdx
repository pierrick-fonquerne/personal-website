## What you will learn

- The chain rule and partial derivatives, intuitively
- Computing the gradient for every weight in the network
- Why "back"-propagation: the direction of dependencies in the graph

Backpropagation is often presented as magic. It isn't: it's the chain rule applied repeatedly, organised to reuse intermediate results. Once the mechanics click, nothing remains obscure.
