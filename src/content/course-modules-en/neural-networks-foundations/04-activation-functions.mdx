## What you will learn

- Why a non-linearity is mandatory in a network
- The four classics: equations, derivatives, shapes
- Choice criteria: vanishing gradient, sparsity, compute cost

Without non-linearity, stacking layers is useless: everything collapses back to a linear combination. The activation function is what gives a network its expressive power.
