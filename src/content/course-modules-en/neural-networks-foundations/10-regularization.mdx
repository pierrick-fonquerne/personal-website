## What you will learn

- The difference between learning and generalising, the overfitting trap
- Splitting the dataset into train, validation and test
- Regularisation techniques: L1, L2, dropout, early stopping

A network that scores 100% on training but 50% on unseen data has learnt nothing useful. This chapter equips you with the tools to diagnose and fix this pitfall, the most common one in practice.
