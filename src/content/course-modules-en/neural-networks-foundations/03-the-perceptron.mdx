## What you will learn

- From the formal neuron to the threshold perceptron
- The learning rule: adjusting weights from errors
- Capabilities and limits (linear separability, Minsky's theorem)

The perceptron is the simplest machine that can learn. We rarely use it today, but understanding how it learns means grasping the intuition behind every modern network.
