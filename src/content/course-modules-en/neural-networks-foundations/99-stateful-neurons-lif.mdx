## What you will learn

- A critique of the stateless neuron paradigm
- The Leaky Integrate-and-Fire (LIF) neuron and its potential V(t)
- Directions toward neuromorphic and spiking neural networks

This annex steps off the main path of the course. It's a detour into what a more biologically grounded neuron could look like: with memory, with temporal dynamics, with spikes.
