## What you will learn

- Learning rate: striking the right step size
- Variants: batch, mini-batch, stochastic
- Classic pitfalls: local minima, plateaus, oscillations

The gradient points along the steepest slope. Gradient descent follows that slope to minimise the loss. All the craft sits in the step size you pick and the sampling strategy you use.
