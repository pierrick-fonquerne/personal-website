## What you will learn

- Momentum and the Nesterov variant: exploiting update inertia
- RMSprop and Adam: per-parameter step adaptation
- Learning-rate scheduling strategies (decay, warmup, cosine)

The pure gradient descent of chapter 9 is rarely used alone in practice. Modern optimisers add cleverness to converge faster and further. This chapter gives you the basics to pick the right tool.
